# Electrical Engineering Projects

 This repository contains PCB and circuit design projects completed during my classes and through personal learning.

## Projects

1. [boost_converter](./boost_converter): Final project for my **Applied Circuits** course, completed with my friends and classmates, involving the design and assembly of a 5 V input to 12 V output converter.

2. [frequency_analyzer_pcb](./frequency_analyzer): Final project for my **Electronics** course, focusing on analysis, simulation, and testing of a frequency analyzer circuit.

3. [star_keychain_pcb](./star_keychain_pcb): A custom star-shaped PCB keychain designed in KiCad as a gift for my younger sisters, and my first independently designed PCB.
